Questions

Good to know

Is there anything for children?

Children are welcome in the main service with their families. Ask an usher about Sunday school.

Can I get personal prayer or counselling?

Yes. Speak to an usher after the service, use the prayer request form, or call/WhatsApp 0741 277 938.

What if I can't attend in person?

Services stream on Facebook and YouTube (@pastorrop), with an online prayer service every evening at 8:00 PM.

Do I have to give?

No. Giving is voluntary and is never a condition for prayer, ministry or welcome.

How do I become a member?

Attend for a few Sundays, then tell an usher or leader you'd like to join. You'll be connected to a fellowship and the membership class.

Can I come to a special programme if I'm new?

Absolutely — everyone is welcome at Holy Communion, healing and deliverance, and anointing services. Check Events for dates.

Devotional · From Pastor Rop

Rejection is often God's redirection

Rejection hurts, but in God's hands it moves us from where we are comfortable to where we are destined to be. When human doors close, we are drawn into a season with God that strengthens the inner person and uncovers gifts we may never have found.

1. A divine change of position

What looks like an ending from a human point of view is often a strategic placement from God's.

  • Joseph was rejected by his brothers and thrown into a pit — on the road to the palace.
  • David was overlooked by his own father, yet God chose him in the field.
  • Jesus became the cornerstone the builders rejected (Psalm 118:22).

2. A season of spiritual solitude

Like a seed buried in the dark before it breaks open and bears fruit, rejection strips away our dependence on people's approval so our faith can mature.

3. Birthing hidden gifts

When people say “no”, we stop waiting for a seat at someone else's table and begin building what God has called us to create — with resilience, creativity and unshakeable faith.

Our story

A house of prayer in Litein

New Life Prayer Center and Church grew from a conviction that God still meets people in prayer, heals the broken and sets captives free. Today the congregation fills a large sanctuary near Litein Shopping Centre every Sunday.

Services blend heartfelt praise, Bible teaching, Holy Communion and prayer for healing and deliverance, in English and Kiswahili. Beyond Sunday, members gather in fellowships, choirs and outreach teams, and our media ministry carries the message online.

🎯

Mission

To raise disciples of Jesus Christ who encounter God, grow in faith and live on mission.

🔭

Vision

A praying, worshipping, Word-centred family where every person finds healing, purpose and belonging.

🤝🏾

Who we serve

Families in Litein and Kericho County, visitors from across Kenya, and friends online in the diaspora.

Senior Pastor

Pastor Rop

Pastor Rop leads New Life Prayer Center and Church with a passion for prayer and the transforming power of the Gospel. He leads Sunday worship, Holy Communion and testimony services, crusades such as Kibugat, and the nightly online prayer service.

His teaching calls believers to stand firm in faith, to see setbacks as God's redirection, and to trust God for healing, deliverance and breakthrough.

“This is the Lord's doing; it is marvellous in our eyes.”Psalm 118:23
